Support

Voice to Text records speech, transcribes it, and lets you edit and organize the resulting notes — all stored locally on your device.

Email oscar.tsai999@gmail.com with a description of what happened, and your device model / iOS version if relevant. I read every message.

Common questions

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your device. There's no account and no cloud sync — if you delete the app, your notes are gone, so make sure to export/copy anything important first.

Why does transcription need microphone / speech recognition permission?

The app uses Apple's Speech Recognition framework to turn your speech into text — both permissions are required for that to work.

Voice to Text 可以錄音、轉成文字，並且讓你編輯、整理這些筆記——所有內容都只存在你的裝置本機。

常見問題

我的錄音存在哪裡？

只存在你的裝置上。沒有帳號、沒有雲端同步——如果你把 App 移除安裝，筆記就會不見，重要的內容記得先匯出或複製起來。

為什麼轉錄需要麥克風／語音辨識權限？

本 App 是用 Apple 的 Speech Recognition framework 把語音轉成文字，這兩個權限都是這個功能運作所必需的。
